DUNGEON CRAWLER DENVER
======================
An arcade of original party games for one crew of friends, inspired in spirit by the Dungeon Crawler Carl books
and tabletop game. Not affiliated with or endorsed by the books' author or publishers.

VOICES
------
System AI, announcers, the yard crew and townsfolk: synthesised with Kokoro TTS (Apache 2.0 licence).


CHARACTERS AND ART
------------------
Crawler models, minions and monsters: generated with Meshy AI, then rigged and fixed up in Blender.
Background people: NPC Forge, built on MakeHuman / MPFB for Blender (MakeHuman assets, CC0).
Paragon characters and props (Muriel, Serath, Zinx and the Paragon props): Epic Games, released free for Unreal Engine.
Item icons and placeholder props: modelled in Blender for this game.

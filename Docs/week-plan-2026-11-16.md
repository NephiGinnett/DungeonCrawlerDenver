# This week (from 2026-11-16)

Zoe's list after the first real session with Anke, plus what that session uncovered. In order: what makes the next
session work, then what makes it fun, then polish. Each item says what "done" looks like, so it can be checked off.

## Already done (2026-11-16, in build 2026-11-16.xxxx on itch)
- **Remote players move on the host.** Walking on locally built ground/props no longer freezes a friend on the host's
  screen, and trucks driven by a friend move on the host. Walking was verified with two copies on one PC; driving
  still needs a real session.
- **Profiles follow the crawler** between PCs (tickets, items, records): verified with two copies on one PC.
- **Fourteener is easier:** climbing costs half the stamina it did (0.74 -> 0.5), hunger drains half as fast, and birds
  only go for climbers a quarter of the way from High Camp to the summit, every 35-55 s instead of 11-18 s, starting
  90 s in.
- **Firewall advice** in the how-to now says to tick both Private and Public.

      into and out of. Inside is safe: monsters do not enter, cannot hit through the bars, and area attacks (blasts,
      firedamp, rockfall, slams) stop at the cage. Done when a monster chasing someone stops at the gate and a blast
      next to the cage does no damage inside it.

- [ ] **Clean end when everyone is down.** If all crawlers are dead, the round ends at once: "Everyone's down" card,

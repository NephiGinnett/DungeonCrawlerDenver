# Prompt for grokbot: find 3D models for Dungeon Crawler Denver

You're sourcing 3D models for **Dungeon Crawler Denver**, a small indie game for a friend group, made in Unreal Engine
5.8. It is a comedy dungeon crawler: Denver has been repossessed by a sinister collections company, and the players are
"crawlers" on a twisted reality TV show run by a smug System AI. The tone is corporate satire meets haunted mine meets
game show:
- Mile High Recovery Solutions is the villain.
- Hiss Cranson is the talk show host.
- There is a haunted 1893 silver mine called the Lucky Tuesday.
- There is a vaporwave city where players repossess cars.

Think *Dungeon Crawler Carl* energy, but **all original**. Never send anything that recreates a specific existing
game's or show's characters, art or logos.

## What I need from you

For each item on the shopping list, find **2 to 4 options**, best first. For every option, give:

1. **Name and a direct link** to the listing.
2. **Price** (free is great; under $30 a pack is fine; flag anything more).
3. **License**: it must allow use in a game we distribute, commercially or not. CC0, CC-BY (give the credit line),
   Fab Standard License, Unreal Marketplace, itch.io and Sketchfab "Standard" or CC-BY are all fine. **No** "editorial
   only", "personal use only", NoDerivatives, or anything ripped from a commercial game.
4. **Format**: FBX, GLB/glTF or a native Unreal (Fab) pack are best. OBJ is fine for props.
5. **Rigged?** For characters: humanoid rig, and which skeleton (UE5 Mannequin, Mixamo, or other). Note whether it
   comes with animations.
6. **Textures**: PBR (colour, normal, roughness/metal) is ideal. Say if it is colour-only.
7. **Poly count**, roughly. Characters ideally under 50k triangles, props under 20k.
8. One line on **why it fits**.

Put it all in a table per section. Skip anything that needs a login just to see the license. Do not buy or download
anything; Zoe will.

## What we already have (don't send these again)

We already own about 30 Fab packs. Stylized sci-fi and fantasy environments are well covered: Ice Station, Modular
Sewer Kit, Synthwave and Cyberpunk cities, Viking Village, Egyptian and jungle temples, Beach House, Diner, NY Subway,
Paragon props, a Weapon Pack, Atris Swords, Monster For Survival Game (slimes, turtles, a chest monster, a beholder),
and Vefects for VFX. We also make one-off characters with Meshy AI and background people with MakeHuman.

**So what we're short on is characters, creatures and specific props.**

## The shopping list, most wanted first

### 1. Hiss Cranson, the talk show host (top priority)
- A sleek, smug **crocodile or alligator man** in a sharp suit. Anthropomorphic, humanoid rig.
- Charismatic game-show-host energy: a big grin, slicked back.
- Stylized or semi-realistic both work.
- Alternatives: a reptile-headed humanoid we could dress in a suit, or a suit outfit that fits a humanoid rig.

### 2. Named NPCs who are wearing someone else's body right now
- **A claims adjuster:** a tired corporate guy in a cheap suit, with a clipboard energy.
- **A site foreman:** hi-vis vest and hard hat, a construction worker.
- **A union steward:** a working-class older man, a flannel or union jacket.
- **A surveyor:** an older, plain field worker, with surveying gear a bonus.
- **Shopkeepers and guards:** generic modern guards (mall cop, security), a prize-counter clerk.
- **The "Nuclear Family":** a retro 1950s dad, mom and kid, ideally a little uncanny. This is a Rocky Flats nuclear
  satire, so hazmat or radiation-glow variants are a bonus.

### 3. Mine monsters for Dead Air (they're currently slimes)
Original creatures for a haunted 1890s silver mine:
- **A lamp-eater:** a gangly thing that snuffs light; think pale cave creature.
- **Slag dogs:** feral dogs made of mine waste and rock, ideally a pack-able quadruped.
- **A Tommyknocker:** a small knocking mine spirit or goblin.
- **The shift boss:** a hulking ghost foreman, big and frightening.
- **Ghost miners:** 1890s miners with lamps on their helmets, for translucent ghosts.

Rigged with animations (idle, walk or run, attack, death) is a huge plus.

### 4. Corporate enemies
Office-satire enemies for a debt-collection company:
- a debt collector,
- a repo man,
- an auditor,
- a "middle manager" mini-boss,
- something monstrous made of paperwork.

Stylized is fine.

### 5. Weapons we have no models for
- **A crossbow:** a cartoony or party-themed one would be perfect.
- **A nail gun, a flare gun and a revolver.**
- **A microphone weapon:** a big glamorous handheld mic, gem-encrusted or crystal ideally. This is the event prize,
  "The Hot Mic".
- **Improvised junk weapons:** a cast-iron skillet, a lacrosse stick, a fire axe, a sledgehammer, a bat, a shovel,
  a rake.

### 6. Armor that fits a body
Our crawlers are stylized humans with very different builds. Look for:
- helmets and hats (a hard hat, a hockey helmet, a crown, a viking helmet, a news anchor's hairpiece is a joke bonus);
- vests and chestplates that are separate meshes;
- modular armor sets, especially ones made for the UE5 Mannequin or Mixamo rigs.

### 7. Loot chests in rarity tiers
Treasure chests or loot boxes with clearly different looks for Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Legendary and Celestial.
A set with matching style and opening animations would be ideal.

### 8. TV studio set pieces
For Hiss's live show:
- a talk show desk and a guest couch;
- studio cameras on pedestals, light rigs and trusses;
- an ON AIR sign;
- audience seating or bleachers;
- a big LED screen frame.

### 9. A spell VFX pack (Unreal Niagara)
We have almost no magic effects: fire, ice, lightning, heal, shield, explosion, buffs. A good free or cheap Niagara
pack would pay for itself.

## How to report

- One table per section, best first.
- At the very end, a short **"if you only grab five things"** list.
- Note anything that was free for a limited time, with its end date.

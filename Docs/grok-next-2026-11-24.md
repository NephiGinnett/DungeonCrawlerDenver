## What changed on 2026-11-23 (so you know where things live)

- **The HUD draws through Slate now** (`UI/DCCHudPaint`).
  - `ADCCHUD` hides AHUD's `DrawRect` / `DrawText` / `GetTextSize` / `DrawLine` / `DrawTexture` and records them; a
    click-through Slate layer repaints them in the look's fonts with rounded panels.
  - Anything that must stay on the Canvas (triangles, additive pictures, render targets) goes inside a
    `FCanvasOnly CanvasOnly(this);` scope. The Canvas is **under** every Slate widget, so a Canvas picture under a
    recorded panel disappears.
- **One look for everything** (`UI/DCCLook`): SETTINGS > STYLE picks Glass, Neon or Broadcast. Subtitles are styled by
  speaker (`DCCLook::VoiceFor`).
- **The Tab menu's CHARACTER page** is a Slate widget (`UI/DCCCharacterSheetWidget`).
- **Item icons** are rendered from each item's mesh by `DCC.MakeItemIcons` into `Content/DCC/UI/ItemIcons/<Id>.png`.
- **Loot box rarities:** `ADCCHUD::DrawTierAura` with textures from `UI/DCCFxTextures`.
- **The OPEN MIC WEEK page:** `UI/DCCEventPanelWidget`, opened by a Tab menu button.
- **Pose bench FIT buttons:** Smaller, Bigger, Slimmer, Wider, Taller, Shorter, Thinner, Deeper, and Auto-fit.
- **Dead Air is PRIMETIME:** `Arcade/DCCHauntPrimetime.cpp` and `UI/DCCHUD_Primetime.cpp`.

### A2. See everything you are wearing, and take it off
Zoe: "I can't seem to easily unequip certain weapons. Like I got a flare gun early and have had a horrible time taking
it off. Maybe give a menu space somewhere functionality to see all equipped items and remove them."
- First find out **why** the flare gun was hard to remove: read how the Inventory tab equips and unequips

  in the log.
- Then: an **EQUIPPED** block at the top of the Character tab: one row per slot (Main Hand, Off Hand, Head, Body,
  Trinket), the item's name and what it gives, and an **UNEQUIP** button on each filled row (a hitbox, like the
  SETTINGS buttons). Empty slots say "empty".
- Done when, in a test run, `DCC.MenuTab 0` shows the block and a dev click on UNEQUIP removes the flare gun (add a
  dev command that clicks a named hitbox if you need one).

### A3. Achievements pop like a System window

bottom right of the screen. And then goes away."

  row queue up; they never overlap.
- Find where an unlock currently shows (`Progression/DCCAchievementComponent.cpp`, the HUD's notices and

- Done when two unlocks fired by a dev command show one after the other, in shots.

### A4. Dead Air: say what is going on, and show more of a solo player's film

### A5. Fourteener: mist over the far mountains

## Part B: bigger pieces

### B1. A SOCIAL tab: your channel, your uploads, your friends
Zoe: "It'd be nice if crawlers just had a social tab in the menu that showed their score (subscribers, however you
want to do it), their 'video uploads' along with the comments on that video and how it did. And then a friends
section that works like DMs. Just fold the current whisper, message, etc functions we have into this section. There
can still be space for it in the chat window, but I think it could all be consolidated off the menu."
- **Keeping uploads.** Each Dead Air post (title, the moment's label, views, likes, comments, shares, the date, the
  game) is saved to that player's arcade profile on the host (`Session/DCCProfile`), newest first, the last 30.
  Profiles sync host <-> clients through `Export` / `MergeFromClient` / `AdoptFromHost`: extend those so uploads travel
  too, and test that a merge never duplicates or drops them. Never write this into the Dungeon Crawler Denver save.
- **CHANNEL** page: subscribers (your call; suggestion: total views / 40 + likes / 10, rounded, shown as "1.2K"),
  total views, your best post. Then **UPLOADS**: a list of posts; clicking one opens it: title, stats, the comments.
- **FRIENDS** page: the crew (the roster, plus anyone you have played with this session), each with an online dot and
  their subscriber count. Clicking a friend opens a DM thread: the existing whispers (`ChatOpen|Whisper...` in
  `UI/DCCHUD.cpp`) shown as a conversation, with a box to write. Party chat and Allies chat move here as two more
  threads at the top.
- The Tab bar gets **SOCIAL** in place of the separate CHATTER / ALLIES / WHISPERS tabs. The always-on chat box stays.
- Done when: a solo Dead Air round's posts appear under UPLOADS after the round, survive a restart, and a whisper sent
  with a dev command shows in that friend's thread.

### B2. Weapons attack the way they look (stab vs swing)
Zoe: "fixing animations for weapons so that they're realistic (a knife having a stab vs an axe having a swing)."
- **Code first, with the clips we have.** Add an attack style to each weapon: **Stab, Slash, Overhead, Blunt, Punch,
  Shoot, Throw**. Derive it in code from the gear table row (name keywords: knife, opener, dagger -> Stab; axe,
  sledge, hammer, mallet -> Overhead; sword, trimmers, machete -> Slash; gun, crossbow, nail -> Shoot; bare hands ->
  Punch) with a small override map for anything that guesses wrong. Do not add a column to the DataTable (that needs
  the editor).
- One table maps style -> clip, in one place (`Characters/DCCCrawlerAnimSet`). The attack plays the style's clip
  (find where `AttackAnimation` is chosen and played, `Multicast_PlayAttack`). Until new clips arrive, use what exists:
  Slash and Overhead `A_DCC_DW_ChargedSlash`, Punch `A_DCC_OA_BoxingWarmup`, Shoot `A_DCC_AO_ArcheryShot`, and Stab the
  fastest of those at a higher play rate. Write in the log which styles are still borrowing a clip.
- **Mixamo clips are coming from Zoe** (she downloads them; see the note at the bottom). Importing and retargeting them
  needs the editor, so **Claude does that part, not you**. Your job is that when they land, adding one is one line in
  the style table.
- Done when a dev command equips a knife, then an axe, then nothing, and the log shows three different styles and clips
  for the same attack.

### B3. Repo Rally: weight, drifting, a longer hook, more gas, and an SSX Tricky vibe
Zoe: "there really isn't enough weight in the driving. I know they're hover vehicles but you really can't drift and
just kind of crash into walls. You also don't interact with enemies long enough. Maybe there should be a 'cutscene'
that plays that shows the moment of magnet connection from an upclose angle? A greater struggle when starting to drag
the car along? Some balancing game? Right now the moment is too short, and there is too little gas to make a round
fulfilling. If we could somehow make the game more like SSX Tricky that would be great. A more fun vibe."
(`Vehicles/DCCRover.*`, `Arcade/DCCArcadeGames.*` (Kart), `UI/DCCHUD_RallyMenus.cpp`, `UI/DCCHUD_VehicleGauges.cpp`.)
Do these **in order**, each tested and logged with its numbers, before the next:
1. **Weight.** Slower to start and to stop, carries momentum through turns, steering tighter at low speed and wider at
   high speed. Put every number in one struct of tunables on the rover with comments, so Zoe can change them.
2. **Drift.** Holding Shift lets the back end slide: less sideways grip, the truck keeps travelling along its old line
   while it turns, and grip comes back gradually when released. A long drift is a skill, not a spin.
3. **Walls.** Hitting a wall at an angle scrapes along it (keep most of the speed along the wall, lose the part into
   it, sparks, a small shake) instead of stopping dead. Only a head-on hit stops you.
4. **More gas.** Rounds should feel full: about +60% fuel to start, gas cans on the streets, and (after step 7) tricks
   that top it up. Check the debt and crew-hunting balance still makes sense and say what changed.
5. **The hook moment.** When the magnet connects: a close-up camera on the clamp for about 1.5 s (sparks, a heavy
   clunk, "CLAMPED!"), on that player's screen only (never slow the world down for everyone in multiplayer).
6. **The struggle.** The first few seconds of a tow are a tug of war: a balance needle the player keeps centred with
   A/D while the mark yanks against it. Keep it centred and the tow locks; lose it and the mark breaks free. Harder for
   marks that were boxed in or driving. Show it on the HUD like a fishing-game bar.
7. **SSX Tricky vibe.** A boost meter that fills from style: long drifts, near-misses with traffic, airtime, wall
   scrapes, clean drops. Trick names pop on screen with a combo multiplier ("NEAR MISS", "DRIFT x3", "BIG AIR").
   When the meter is full, a super boost: the screen streaks, the music lifts, everything is faster for a few seconds.
   The multiplier at a drop multiplies the cash.
Done for each step when shots and a log line show it working. Keep network play in mind: the truck must still look
right to the other players (test with two windows as in `Docs/grok-next-2026-11-17.md` task 5 once step 3 is done).

### B4. Fourteener: climb round corners, and rocks that are held up
Zoe: "Please make Fort Teener so that you can wrap around walls and edges while climbing. Please fix some of the rock
slabs so that they're more supported by edges. Right now it feels like they're just floating protruding rock faces.
Especially the rock feature that has a thin layer of dirt through the middle. It currently serves as an obvious mesh
failure."
- **Round corners.** Climbing sideways to the end of a face, the crawler follows the rock round an outside corner (and
  into an inside one) instead of stopping or falling: trace ahead and round the edge, turn to the new face's normal,
  keep climbing. Find the side-to-side climbing code in `Characters/DCCCharacterBase.cpp` (the hold-to-climb section).
  `DCC.ClimbTest` (header in `Arcade/DCCClimbTest.cpp`) must still pass with no new jumps or stuck time; add a corner
  to its route if you can.
- **Floating slabs.** First **find** them: a `DCC.Peek` tour round the mountain (shots every ~15 degrees at three
  heights), then list each slab that sticks out with nothing under it and the feature with the dirt layer through it,
  with its position. Show Zoe the shots in the log. Then fix each: sink it into the face, add a supporting rock under
  it, or remove the broken piece. If the mountain's pieces come from `Content/` meshes you cannot edit, fix it by
  placement (code), not by editing the mesh.
- Done when before/after shots of every slab you listed are in the log, and the climb test still passes.

### B5. The Character tab as a character sheet, and glass UI
Zoe: "Make the Character tab a little more professional and nice. Make the UI everywhere a little more crystal glass
and impressive, or make several different stylized UIs that the player can unlock. The character tab can be stylized
more like a character sheet from DnD, but the equipment should be much easier to influence. Spells and weapons too. It
needs better organizing to be an RPG game."
- **The sheet.** Laid out like a tabletop character sheet: the five scores in boxes, each with its modifier in a

  "put on belt" button, since the belt is how they are used now).
- **Glass.** `UI/DCCMenuStyle.*` is where the menu look lives. Make the default a frosted crystal look: panels a deep
  translucent tint with a bright 1 px top edge and a soft lighter band along the top, thin light borders, a faint inner
  shadow, consistent padding. Apply it to every Tab page, the panels, and the popups from A3.
- **Unlockable styles.** Two more looks beside Crystal: **Neon Arcade** and **Tabletop** (parchment and ink). Pick in
  SETTINGS. For now all three are available; leave a clear hook (a profile cosmetic id per style) so they can be put
  behind achievements or tickets later. Do not decide the unlock costs: ask Zoe.
- Done when shots of the Character tab, Inventory and SETTINGS exist in all three styles.

## Note for Zoe: Mixamo (from Claude)
Yes, it is worth downloading. Mixamo (mixamo.com, free with an Adobe account) has exactly the motions B2 needs, and it
is free to use in games. It is a sign-in site, so you have to do the download yourself. In short: sign in, keep the
default **Y Bot**, search each term below, tick **In Place** where offered, download as **FBX Binary, 30 fps, Without
Skin**, and save into `D:\Assets\Mixamo\<style>\`. Download one extra **Y Bot T-pose With Skin** as the retarget
source. Search terms: stab, sword slash, great sword slash, standing melee attack downward, standing melee attack
horizontal, punching, hook punch, sword and shield block, standing draw arrow, pistol, firing rifle, throw, hit
reaction, dying. Claude then retargets them onto the crew's skeleton and adds them to B2's table.

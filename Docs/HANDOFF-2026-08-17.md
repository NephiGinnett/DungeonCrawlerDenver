## Fixed

### The five `HealthBarWidget` Blueprint errors — root-caused to my own code

`ADCCEnemyCharacter::BeginPlay` was calling `DestroyComponent()` on the legacy
`WBP_EnemyHealthBar`. Each subclass's EventGraph still holds a reference and calls
`SetVisibility` on it, so destroying it left five Blueprints reading garbage every
PIE start.

Now **hidden, not destroyed**. Costs nothing the destroy saved — an invisible
component is skipped by the renderer, and with its tick off the per-frame Blueprint
recompute (the actual expense) is gone too. The reference stays valid, so the
Blueprint's own `SetVisibility(false)` becomes a harmless no-op.

### Menu UX

- **Action verbs now close the conversation behind them** — Recruit, Follow Me,
  Hold Position, Revive and Loot all dismiss the menu. Standing in a menu after
  recruiting somebody was the specific "stuck" feeling.
- **Talking mid-fight now takes two presses.** With a living hostile inside 1600
  units, the first `E` arms a prompt (*"Press E again to talk to CJ"*, drawn
  centre-screen) and the second opens the menu. Out of combat it opens immediately,
  as before.
- **Reviving and looting are exempt from that gate** — both are things you do
  *because* the fight is going badly, and a confirmation step on picking a friend
  up off the floor would be actively cruel.
- Tunable: `CombatProximityRadius`, `TalkConfirmSeconds` on the character.

## The Balloon Payment — built, needs art and placement

`UDCCBossComponent` now supports a multi-phase, levitating, swarming charge.

| Field | Voldemortgage's value | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `PhaseThresholds` | `0.75, 0.45, 0.20` | Three charges instead of one. Highest first |
| `ChargeDurationSeconds` | `7` | Down from 9 — there are three now |
| `ChargeLiftHeight` | `650` | He floats up out of melee reach |
| `ChargeDamageResistance` | `0.6` | Scales *with inflation*, so hitting him early is worth more than hitting him late |
| `MinionClass` | `DebtEater` | Spawns on the ground beneath him, not at altitude |
| `MinionSpawnInterval` | `2.2s` | First one arrives after 0.5s so the channel reads as dangerous immediately |
| `MinionsPerPhaseBonus` | `1` | Later phases swarm thicker |

The balloon is a sphere component on the boss that scales `0.5 → 6.0` with charge
progress and rises with its own size so it never clips through his head. Driven off
the **replicated** `ChargeProgress`, so it inflates on every client.

He **settles back to the ground** on both resolve and cancel — the deflate-and-thump
is the party's reward for breaking the channel, and it has to be visible from the
floor to land as one.

**Backwards compatible:** bosses with an empty `PhaseThresholds` keep using the old
single `ChargeHealthThreshold` exactly as before, so the TestBoss is unaffected.

## Added after the handoff was written (still needs the same build)

### Test console commands — better than hardcoding Fire Fingers

Rather than baking Fire Fingers onto the player Blueprint (which MCP was down for
anyway, and which would have been a permanent change made for a temporary reason),
there are now three exec commands. Open `~` in PIE:

```
DCCGrantSkill FireFingers 3
DCCEquip SodaGun
DCCRefill
```

- `DCCGrantSkill <id> <rank>` learns **any** of the 40 skill rows, or re-ranks one
  already known. Refuses ids that are not in the table rather than adding a dead
  hotbar slot that fails every activation.
- `DCCEquip <id>` equips any equipment row.
- `DCCRefill` tops up health, mana and stamina.

All three are server-guarded and route through RPCs, so they work from a client in
a multiplayer test rather than only for the host.

### Ranged weapons — the system, and three items

**We had no ranged weapons and no way to have any:** `FDCCEquipmentDef` had no range
field, so nothing could reach past the character's fixed 200-unit melee.

`AttackRangeOverride` now exists on equipment, and `GetEffectiveAttackRange()` on the
character prefers the longest reach among equipped items. No projectile is simulated
— the shot resolves instantly like every other attack, matching how the ranged
*skills* already behave rather than inventing a second combat path.

For melee we were already fine: **Tenderiser Mallet (d8), Chef's Knife (d6), Cast
Iron Skillet (d6 +1 Evade), Fire Axe (d10), Wall Sword (d10)**, plus a shield and a
tray for the off-hand.

Three ranged items added to `Docs/DT_DCCEquipment.csv`:

| Well Bottle, Underhand | d6 | 850 | `Cave_Camp/SM_bottle` |
